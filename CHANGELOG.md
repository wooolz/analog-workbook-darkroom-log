## Edition 1.0 – November 2026

First public edition (English).

- **Workbook interior** in three bindings and two sizes (A5, A4):
  - ring binding, 80 pages, 3 mm bleed (120 session blocks)
  - booklet, 40 pages, 3 mm bleed (50 session blocks)
  - online/self-print, 40 pages + cover pages (light, dark or safelight-red cover), no bleed, clickable contents
- **Content:** title page with contents, 2-page how-to with example entries and a QR code to the extended how-to, setup tables (processes, enlargers & lenses, printing papers), project index, session index, learnings pages, session log (two blocks per page; A4 with extra ROLL/NEG, PAPER and SIZE columns), quick references (f-stop timing, print size change, RA-4 casts, contrast grades, zone system), general knowledge notes.
- **Extended how-to:** 2 pages, A4, online only.
- **Covers:** dark, light and safelight-red theme; print (3 mm bleed) and online/self-print (no bleed); A5 and A4; one-piece covers (back · spine · front) for booklet, softcover, hardcover and ring binding.
- **Self-print booklet:** A5 online version imposed two-up on A4 landscape for duplex printing and stapling.
- Print-ready PDFs without transparency.
- Every file as PDF and Word (.docx).
